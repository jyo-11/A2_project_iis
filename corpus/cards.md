If your card is lost or stolen, please freeze it straight away in the app:

**To freeze your card:**

1. Tap **Cards**.
2. Tap **Freeze card**.

**To order a new card :**

1. Tap **Cards**.
2. Tap **Replace card**.

Your new card will arrive in about **5 working days**.
