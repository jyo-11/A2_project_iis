If your card is lost or stolen, please **freeze it straight away** in the app.

**To freeze your card:**

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Cards**.
3. Tap **Freeze card**.

**To order a new card:**

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Cards**.
3. Tap **Replace card**.

Your new card will arrive in about **5 working days**.
